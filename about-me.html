Hi there! My name is Davies Kubona, and I am an IT student at Triumphant College. Ever since I began my IT diploma journey back in 2024, I have been deeply hooked on the revolutionary power and limitless capabilities of Artificial Intelligence.

But writing code isn't my only calling—I am also deeply passionate about the art of acting. Experiencing firsthand what performers face led me to a crucial realization: why not combine these two worlds?

That intersection is exactly why StageMind was born. My mission is to integrate AI into the performing arts ecosystem, creating innovative digital tools that help young, aspiring actors sharpen their craft, master their scenes, and excel at every single audition.
